// 001 🏆 Winner — Paris Builds (YC × Unaite)

VIGIE — Robot Safety Watchdog

36h hackathon · won a Y Combinator interview

An external runtime safety layer for learning-based robots. VIGIE watches the robot and its workspace through environment cameras, detects dangerous situations, and can pause, hold and resume any robots run when the scene becomes unsafe. Built because robot safety shouldn't depend on the policy alone. Built in 36 hours with a live demo harness interfaced with LeRobot and OpenArm.
