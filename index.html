About

I'm a final-year engineering student at École Centrale de Lyon, specialising in automation, machine learning and computer vision — with a Master's in Automation.

My work lives at the intersection of perception and control: giving robots the ability to understand a scene, act on it, and do so safely. // 001 🏆 Winner — Paris Builds (YC × Unaite)

VIGIE — Robot Safety Watchdog

36h hackathon · won a Y Combinator interview

An external runtime safety layer for learning-based robots. VIGIE watches the robot and its workspace through environment cameras, detects dangerous situations, and can pause, hold and resume a real OpenARM / LeRobot run when the scene becomes unsafe — because robot safety shouldn't depend on the policy alone. Built in 36 hours with a live demo harness interfaced with LeRobot.

// 003 Robotics

LeJunior — Vision Pick & Place

SO-101 · YOLO11 · ACT policy

A vision-guided pick-and-place pipeline: an SO-101 arm picks grey PVC rings and sorts them into a 4×2 box, slot by slot. A fine-tuned YOLO11 detector feeds a slot grid, an ACT policy runs motor control at 30 Hz, and a task state machine handles grasp verification, retries and occlusion.
